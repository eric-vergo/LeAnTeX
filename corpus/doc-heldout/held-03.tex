\section*{Counting lattice paths}
How many paths lead from $(0,0)$ to $(m,n)$ using unit steps right and up? Each path is a word of length $m+n$ with $m$ letters R and $n$ letters U, so the number is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binom}
\binom{m+n}{m}=\frac{(m+n)!}{m!\,n!}.
\end{equation}
For $m=n$ this is the central binomial coefficient, and by Stirling's formula
\[
\binom{2n}{n}\sim\frac{4^n}{\sqrt{\pi n}}.
\]

\subsection*{Paths staying below the diagonal}
The number of such paths from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$ that never go above the diagonal is the Catalan number
\begin{equation}
C_n=\frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}.
\end{equation}
The first few values are collected below.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|cccccc}
$n$ & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5\\ \hline
$C_n$ & 1 & 1 & 2 & 5 & 14 & 42
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Comparing with \eqref{eq:binom} shows $C_n$ is always an integer, since $C_n=\binom{2n}{n}-\binom{2n}{n+1}$.

\subsubsection*{A recurrence}
Splitting a path at its first return to the diagonal gives
\begin{align*}
C_{n+1}&=\sum_{k=0}^{n}C_kC_{n-k},\\
C_0&=1.
\end{align*}
