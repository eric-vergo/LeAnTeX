\section{Quadrature}
We wish to approximate $I=\int_0^1 e^{-x^2}\,dx$. The composite trapezoidal rule with $n$ panels gives
\begin{equation}
T_n=\frac h2\bigl(f_0+2f_1+\dots+2f_{n-1}+f_n\bigr),\qquad h=\frac1n,
\end{equation}
with error $O(h^2)$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|r|l|l|}
\hline
$n$ & $T_n$ & error\\ \hline
2 & 0.7313703 & $1.4\times10^{-2}$\\
4 & 0.7429840 & $3.6\times10^{-3}$\\
8 & 0.7458656 & $9.0\times10^{-4}$\\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The error drops by a factor of about four each time $n$ doubles, as predicted.

\section{Newton's method}
To solve $f(x)=0$ iterate
\[
x_{k+1}=x_k-\frac{f(x_k)}{f'(x_k)}.
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item Near a simple root convergence is quadratic.
\item Near a multiple root it is only linear.
\item The method may diverge from a poor starting point.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Warning.} Always bound the number of iterations.\footnote{A fixed cap such as one hundred is typical.}
