In a paragraph with a sum $\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$ and an integral $\int_0^1 f$ the lines are spaced more widely, since the limits sit beside the operators here. A limit such as $\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n$ and a fraction $\frac{a+b}{c}$ in the text do the same, as do $\sum_{k=0}^{m} \frac{1}{k+1}$ and $\int_a^b g$, which every typesetting system must place with care. More words follow so that the paragraph runs over several lines and shows the spacing.

A second paragraph with $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\sum_{i=1}^n i^2$ and $\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x}$ and also $\int_0^\infty e^{-x}$ and the plain $x^2+y^2$ in a line. Then ordinary words to finish the paragraph, long enough for several lines of text.
