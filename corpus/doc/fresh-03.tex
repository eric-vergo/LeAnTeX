\section{Overview}
The library served more visitors this quarter than in any quarter since the
renovation. The reading room remained popular with students, while the
children's section hosted a weekly story hour.

\section{Events}
\begin{description}
\item[Story hour] Held every Tuesday; attendance averaged twenty-two children.
\item[Book sale] A two-day sale raised funds for new shelving.
\item[Lecture series] Three evening talks on regional history, each followed
  by refreshments.
\end{description}

\section{Staffing}
\begin{flushleft}
Two part-time assistants were hired in February. The head librarian
expresses gratitude to the volunteers, whose contribution is immeasurable.
\end{flushleft}
