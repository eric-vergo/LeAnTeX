A display after a long line of text uses the ordinary skips above and below it, since the formula starts to the left of where the text line ended:
\[ \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^k y^{n-k} = (x+y)^n. \]
A short line
\[ e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 \]
takes the short skips. A numbered equation
\begin{equation}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\end{equation}
and a matrix
\[ A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det A = ad - bc, \]
and cases
\[ |x| = \begin{cases} x & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{cases} \]
close the document.
