A typesetting system takes a description of a document and decides where every character goes. It breaks paragraphs into lines and stacks the lines into pages.

\begin{tabular}{l p{4cm}}
First & A paragraph of text in a narrow column, long enough that it breaks into several lines and hyphenates some of its longer words, such as typesetting and incomprehensible.

Second paragraph in the same cell, which also breaks. \\
Second & Another cell with extraordinarily lengthy vocabulary, internationalization, and characteristically unhyphenated pieces. \\
\end{tabular}

Lines of a paragraph are chosen together rather than one at a time, so that the spacing is as even as the words allow and few hyphens appear.
