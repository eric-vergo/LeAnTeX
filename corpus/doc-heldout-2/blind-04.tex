\section{Setting}
Consider the system $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ with
\[
  A=\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0\\ -1 & 2 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & 2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  \mathbf{b}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
This is the discrete second-difference matrix, which appears whenever one discretises $-u''=f$ on an interval with Dirichlet boundary conditions.

\section{Determinant and inverse}
Expanding along the first row,
\begin{equation}
  \det A=\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0\\ -1 & 2 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & 2\end{vmatrix}
  = 2\cdot3-(-1)(-2)=4 .
\end{equation}
Since $\det A\ne0$, the matrix is invertible, and
\[
  A^{-1}=\frac14\begin{pmatrix}3&2&1\\2&4&2\\1&2&3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hence $\mathbf{x}=A^{-1}\mathbf{b}=(1,1,1)^{\mathsf T}$.

\section{Eigenvalues}
The eigenvalues are $\lambda_k = 2-2\cos\frac{k\pi}{4}$ for $k=1,2,3$, that is
$2-\sqrt2$, $2$ and $2+\sqrt2$. Their product is $(2-\sqrt2)\cdot2\cdot(2+\sqrt2)=4$, in agreement with the determinant. The condition number is therefore
\[
  \kappa(A)=\frac{2+\sqrt2}{2-\sqrt2}\approx 5.83 .
\]
