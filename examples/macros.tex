A \term{norm} on a vector space $V$ over $\R$ is a function $\norm{\cdot} : V \to \R$ with
$\norm{x} \ge 0$, $\norm{\lambda x} = |\lambda| \, \norm{x}$ and
$\norm{x + y} \le \norm{x} + \norm{y}$; macros defined in the text work in its formulas too.
