ages from Lines A. Writer 3 March 2025 Abstract A typesetting system takes a description of a document and decides where every character goes. It breaks paragraphs into lines, stacks the lines into pages and places headings, notes and figures where the reader expects them. Each of these steps follows rules that can be written down precisely, and a good program follows them exactly. The amount of stretch is small, and most of it goes where a reader will not notice. Notes at the foot of a page are collected separately from the main text.
