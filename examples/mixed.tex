Euler found that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$, and the identity
$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ joins five constants. For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\alpha > 0$ the
Gaussian integral is
\[ \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\alpha x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{\alpha}}, \]
and the binomial theorem reads
\[ (x + y)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^k y^{n-k}. \]
A function defined by cases, $f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} x^2 & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0
\end{array} \right.$, is continuous; the sets $\mathcal{F}$, $\mathfrak{g}$ and
$\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w} = \lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert \, \lVert \mathbf{w} \rVert \cos\theta$
appear in the same line as text.
