\newcommand{\R}{\mathbb{R}}
For every $x \in \R$ we have $x^2 \ge 0$, and the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is
\( n^2 \): \[ \sum_{k=1}^{n} (2k - 1) = n^2 . \] The formula above is displayed; the text goes
on after it without indentation, as in LaTeX's paragraphs.
